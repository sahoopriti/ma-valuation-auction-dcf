# ma-valuation-auction-dcf
Buy-side M&amp;A valuation case using DCF analysis to assess intrinsic value under auction dynamics, competitor bids, and short-term operational disruption.
1. Problem Statement:
  WorldWide Brewing Co. is in the final stage of submitting a binding offer for the acquisition of HappyHour Co. A recent fire at one of HappyHour Co.’s packaging facilities introduced short-term operational and    financial uncertainty. Additionally, market reports suggest a competing bidder may offer more than $650mm, intensifying auction dynamics.
